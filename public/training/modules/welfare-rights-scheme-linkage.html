The Foundation

One system, from map to household

Our urban work rests on a single spine: a settlement is mapped, streets are enumerated, households and individuals are line-listed, and every scheme (CMCHIS, OAP, ECP) reads from and writes back to the same profile.

Frappe

Settlement Mapping

Every intervention begins by defining the settlement — its boundary, its selection status, and its assigned Area Coordinator.

  • Boundary + metadataWard, zone, AC allocation, phase-1 status
  • Lifecycle-awareDraft → deprecate → rename → merge/split, all tracked
  • Filtered downstreamOnly "selected" settlements flow into field workplans
Frappe

Street Mapping

Streets are the operational unit — each carries its own CO/AC allocation, container assignment, and selection status.

  • Street List – WRPBridges settlements to households; drives CO scoping
  • Selection statusFilters out "No" and "Yes, but not for Phase 1"
  • Container-linkedEvery street is grouped into a container for allocation & reporting

Workflow

How the 30-Visit Logic Works

Each CO has a rolling workplan of 30 households at a time — drawn from three pools of 9 plus 3 buffer — to keep field workload predictable and prioritised.

Pool 1
9 fresh enrolments
Pool 2
9 pending documents
Pool 3
9 follow-ups
Buffer
3 urgent / grievance
  • Auto-refillAs a case closes, the pool pulls the next candidate by SLA priority
  • SLA-awareAging households jump to the top of pool 3
